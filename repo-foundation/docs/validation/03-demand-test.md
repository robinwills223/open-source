# Testing Quantitative Demand with a Simple Landing Page

## Purpose

Once customer interviews confirm a real, recurring pain point, a lightweight demand test determines whether strangers will take concrete action when presented with your proposed solution.

---

## 1. Core Elements of an Effective Landing Page

Keep the page focused, lightweight, and honest. Avoid bloated templates:

1. **Clear, Benefit-Driven Headline:** State the specific problem you eliminate in 10 words or fewer.
   *(Example: "Generate compliant freelance invoices and get paid in minutes, not hours.")*
2. **Pain Statement:** Three bullet points describing the exact headaches interviewees shared with you.
3. **Proposed Solution Preview:** A brief 2-sentence description or annotated mockup showing the workflow.
4. **Single Call to Action (CTA):**
   - *Option A (Early Interest):* Join early-access beta list (email capture).
   - *Option B (Strong Signal):* Pre-order deposit or refundable reservation (\$10–\$50).

---

## 2. Metrics to Measure

- **Visitor Traffic:** Total unique visitors from relevant communities (target 100–300 visitors).
- **CTA Conversion Rate:**
  - Email signup for beta: Target **5% to 15%** conversion.
  - Paid reservation or deposit: Target **1% to 3%** conversion.
- **Bounce Rate & Time on Page:** Indicates whether visitors are actually reading the value proposition.

---

## 3. Honest Limits of Landing Page Tests

> [!WARNING]
> A landing page measures **initial curiosity and positioning resonance**. It does **NOT** measure:
> - User retention (will they come back on day 30?).
> - Core product utility (does the app actually work as advertised?).
> - Churn or ongoing willingness to pay recurring subscriptions.
> Treat landing page conversion as permission to start building a minimal prototype, not proof of long-term business viability.

---

## 4. Legal & Ethical Communication Standards

- **State Product Stage Clearly:** Do not create a fake checkout or pretend software exists when it does not. State clearly: *"Currently in private development. Join our early beta cohort launching [Month]."*
- **Privacy Notice & Consent:** If collecting emails or names, provide a clear privacy policy link stating how contact information is stored and used. Never sell or spam email lists. Comply with applicable regional data protection rules (such as GDPR or CCPA).

# Discovery Interview Script & Note-Taking Framework

## Interviewing Principles: The Mom Test Rule

People will naturally lie to protect your feelings if you ask them about your idea. To get genuine data:
- **Talk about their past behavior, never your future product.**
- **Listen 80% of the time, speak 20% of the time.**
- **Never pitch, demo, or defend your solution during discovery.**

---

## 1. Word-for-Word Question Script

### Opening (1 minute)
> *"Thanks for making time today! We are researching how professionals in your industry manage [problem domain, e.g. client invoicing and inventory tracking]. We are not selling anything today; we just want to understand your daily workflow."*

### Exploring Past Behavior (10 minutes)
1. **Recent experience:**
   > *"Can you walk me through the last time you had to [perform task / deal with problem]?"*
2. **Current workaround:**
   > *"What tools, spreadsheets, or processes did you use to get that done?"*
3. **Friction and bottlenecks:**
   > *"What was the hardest or most frustrating part about doing it that way?"*
4. **Quantifying cost:**
   > *"Roughly how much time did that take you or your team? Did it hold up any other work?"*
5. **Existing attempts to solve:**
   > *"Have you looked into other software or tools to solve this? If so, why didn't you stick with them?"*

### Wrapping Up (3 minutes)
6. **Referrals:**
   > *"Who else on your team or in your network wrestles with this same headache whom we should talk to?"*

---

## 2. Questions to NEVER Ask

| Prohibited Question | Why It Fails | What to Ask Instead |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| *"Would you use an app that did X?"* | Invites hypothetical optimism. Everyone says yes to free imaginary apps. | *"How do you currently handle X today?"* |
| *"Would you pay \$20/month for this?"* | People cannot predict future spending accurately in conversation. | *"What software tools do you currently pay for to manage this workflow?"* |
| *"Don't you hate how slow current tools are?"* | Leading question that feeds the answer you want to hear. | *"What is your experience using your current system?"* |

---

## 3. Note-Taking Guidelines

1. **Record verbatim quotes:** Capture exact words and phrasing. Founders often use jargon; customers use practical slang. Use their exact words in your future marketing copy.
2. **Log active workarounds:** Record specific tool names (e.g., *"Google Sheets with 14 tabs + Zapier"*).
3. **Capture numbers:** Hours per week, dollars spent, staff dedicated.
4. **Immediate debrief:** Spend 5 minutes immediately after the call filling out the structured template in [interview-notes.md](file:///repo-foundation/templates/validation/interview-notes.md).
